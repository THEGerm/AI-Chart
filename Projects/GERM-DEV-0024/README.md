# GERM-DEV-0024: Squawk & Survive

Can you survive one week as a gull? A single-file browser game for ages 9 to 99: 7 days, 3 actions a day, four stats, three gull species, six locations and ten survival principles. Win by ending Day 7 with Flock Rep and Confidence both at 55 or more.

- [Play the game](SeagullSurvival.html) (vanilla HTML, CSS and JavaScript; Google Fonts only; no storage, no network calls)
- [Requirements and rules](SquawkAndSurvive_Rules.md) (stats, actions, species, events, ranks, balance)
