Senet

The board game of ancient Egypt, played for some 3,000 years. Nobody knows the full rules. Pick a scholar's reconstruction and play it; every rule shows how firm its evidence is.

 

Rules in play

Evidenced an ancient board, set, picture or text shows it. Inferred a scholar's reading of ancient material. Invented a modern rule added to make the game work.
