# Senet rules: what each rests on

Certainty runs 95-100 for several independent ancient sources, 80-94 for one ancient source or several with minor disagreement, 55-79 for an inference with a direct textual or pictorial hint, 30-54 for an inference by analogy or from indirect material, 10-29 for an invention with a weak ancient hint, and 0-9 for an invention with no ancient basis. Basis says whether an ancient source shows the rule (evidenced), ancient material points to it without stating it (inferred), or a modern author supplied it (invented). Where every option is invented, the rule column gives the version with the nearest ancient hint, or Kendall's when there is none; the other options column lists the rest. Sources are in full in [research.md](research.md).
